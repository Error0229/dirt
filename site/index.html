Dirt

Do I Remember That? A place to drop a thought in under two seconds, before it is gone.

Dirt is a note app for the thoughts that pass in a moment: a shortcut from anywhere on Windows, a widget, a tile and a capture sheet on Android, and dirt "my thought" in a terminal. Notes are plain text, organised with #tags, and kept on your device first.

With an account, your notes sync between your devices. They are encrypted on the device before they leave it, under a key the server never has: the server stores only ciphertext it cannot read.

What it runs on

  • Windows 10 and 11: the desktop app and the dirt command.
  • Android 7.0 and later.

Signing in

You can use Dirt without an account; your notes then stay on the device. To sync, you sign in with a code sent to your email address, or with Google. Signing in proves who you are; reading your notes on a new device also needs one of your devices that is already in, or your recovery phrase.
