# Dirt 0.1.0

Dirt ("Do I Remember That?") catches a thought in under two seconds, typed or
said, and keeps it as a plain note with `#tags`. Your notes sync between your
Windows computer and your Android phone, encrypted on your devices: the
server keeps them and cannot read them.

This is the first release, for a few people. It is offered for **Windows**
and **Android** only. There are no builds for macOS or Linux.

## Install on Windows

1. Download `Dirt-0.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit).
2. Run it. The installer is **not signed**, so Windows does not know who made
   it:
   - your browser may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded": choose
     **Keep** (in Edge, under **…**, then **Show more**, **Keep anyway**);
   - SmartScreen then says "Windows protected your PC": press **More info**,
     then **Run anyway**.
3. It installs for you alone, without asking for an administrator, into
   `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Dirt`, with Dirt in the Start menu and on the
   desktop. WebView2, which Dirt draws in, is part of Windows 11 and of an
   up-to-date Windows 10; the installer fetches it only if it is missing.

Dirt stays in the tray when its window is closed. **Ctrl+Alt+N** opens quick
capture over whatever you are doing; held, it listens instead.

Your notes, keys and settings are not in the program's folder: they are in
`%APPDATA%\dirt` and in Windows' Credential Manager. Uninstalling Dirt
(Settings → Apps) leaves them. To install a newer version, choose **Quit
Dirt** from the tray first.

**The command line**, `dirt-cli-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip`, holds `dirt.exe`. Put
it in a folder on your `PATH` and run `dirt --help`. On one computer the
command line and the app are one device: they share the notes and the
sign-in.

## Install on Android

1. On the phone, download `Dirt-0.1.0-android-arm64-v8a.apk` in the browser
   (Android 7 or later; any phone of the last several years).
2. Open it. Android asks whether the browser (or the Files app) may install
   apps: allow it, go back, and press **Install**. If Play Protect says it
   does not know the app, choose **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
3. Dirt is on the home screen as **Dirt**. Long-press the home screen to add
   its two widgets (the bar, and the pile), and edit Quick Settings to add
   its **New note** tile.

**If you had a development build of Dirt** (it was called "DirtMobile" and
had no icon): to Android this release is another app, beside that one, and
the two do not share notes on the phone. Before you uninstall the old one:

1. Open it, sign in if you had not, and let it bring in the notes it kept
   while signed out.
2. In Settings → Sync, wait for "Everything on this device has reached the
   server".
3. Uninstall it. Its widgets and tile go with it.
4. Install this one, sign in, and let it in from another device or with
   your recovery phrase.

Later releases install over this one and keep its notes.

## Signing in

Settings → Account → **Sign in** opens the sign-in page in the browser. The
first device you sign in on makes the account. Every other device is let in
by one that is already in: both show the same seven pictures, and you say
whether they match. Make a **recovery phrase** too (Settings → Devices): if
you lose your only device, those 24 words are the only way back to your
notes. Dirt works signed out as well; your notes then stay on that device.

## What is in this version

- **Writing.** A blank page, always ready: the first character makes the
  note, and it saves itself. Esc puts it away. Plain text, as long as you
  like; `#tags` anywhere in it.
- **Capture from anywhere.** On Windows, quick capture (Ctrl+Alt+N) and the
  tray. On Android, the capture sheet from the bar widget, the Quick
  Settings tile (over the lock screen too), the app's shortcuts, Android's
  note-taking role, and "Share" from any app, for text and pictures.
- **Voice.** Hold the mic, speak, let go: the recording is kept at once and
  its words follow, made on your own device by a speech model shipped in the
  app. Nothing you say is sent anywhere but your encrypted note. On Android,
  **Dirt Talk** can be set on the phone's side key or Quick Tap.
- **Finding.** Search by words on every key, tags (all of them, or any), and
  the list in layers by when you wrote. The pile of sand under the list, and
  on Android a widget of it, is every thought you have kept.
- **Putting away.** Archive or delete with one key or one swipe, and
  **Undo**. A deleted note can be brought back for thirty days.
- **Files.** Attach pictures, PDFs, sounds, videos and other files (up to
  10 MB each, 20 a note), encrypted like the notes; preview them in the app,
  with zoom and a swipe between them.
- **Sync and devices.** Encrypted end to end; devices let in by comparing
  pictures, or the recovery phrase; devices listed and removable; the
  account's key changeable; the account deletable.
- **Export.** Every note as text, JSON or Markdown, with its files beside it.
- **The command line.** `dirt "a thought"`, `dirt list`, `dirt show`,
  `dirt edit`, search, tags, files, export, and everything about the account
  and its devices.

## What has not been tried by hand

Everything else was tried by hand on Windows 11 and on a Pixel phone, or run
by automated tests on an emulator and on test copies of the app. These were
not tried by a person yet, so they may not work as they should. The codes in
brackets are the items of `docs/MANUAL_CHECKLIST.md`.

**Screen readers.** Dirt is built to be used with Narrator and TalkBack, but
nobody has tried it with them: moving through the list, archiving, syncing,
the files of a note, the voice notes' announcements (D35, D87, D110, D117;
A41, A62, A71, A85).

**Voice on Windows, in the unusual cases.** The microphone turned off in
Windows' privacy settings, or no microphone at all (D79, D80); quitting Dirt
just after speaking (D81); talking for more than ten minutes (D84); closing
the window while it listens (D89: this went wrong, was fixed, and has not
been tried again); signing out while it listens (D90); the installed app
reading speech with no network (D85).

**Elsewhere on Windows.**
- Dragging files from Explorer onto the window (D18: this went wrong, was
  fixed, and has not been tried again).
- The tray menu by right-click (D3), and the tray icon on light and dark
  taskbars, at other scales and accents (D104).
- What quick capture does when a note cannot be saved (D73), or when the
  device is signed out while it is open (D74).
- Exporting with the network off (D25); Win+Esc in a note (D46).
- Showing pictures read ahead in the preview, which came after the
  sign-off (D121).

**Android, on real phones.**
- The lock screen's shortcut on a Samsung, and Android's note-taking role
  (L6).
- Capturing with the battery saver on, or after days unused (L8); the
  widget's bar after the phone has stopped Dirt (L9).
- **Dirt Talk** on the side key or Quick Tap (L12).
- The preview's swipe between pictures after its last changes, which came
  after the sign-off (A88, A89).

**The command line.** Reading, choosing and editing notes by number or by
words (`dirt show`, `dirt edit`, `dirt delete`) came after the sign-off and
has not been tried by hand, nor has `dirt edit` with Notepad already open or
with the same note open in the app (C30 to C35).

**These release builds themselves.** The installer and the APK were built
and checked by the release workflow, and not yet installed by a person
(R1, R2).

## Not offered

- **macOS and Linux.** The code builds there, but nothing has been tried
  enough to offer it, and there are no builds.
- **A signed Windows installer.** Hence SmartScreen's warning.
- **Other languages.** Dirt is in English, and its voice understands
  English.
